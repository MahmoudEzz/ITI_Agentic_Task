NORTHFIELD DIGITAL — JOB DESCRIPTION
Role: Data Analyst
Team: Business Intelligence & Analytics
Location: Cairo, Egypt (hybrid — 3 days on-site)
Reports to: Analytics Manager

ABOUT THE ROLE

The Business Intelligence & Analytics team turns Northfield's product and operational data into decisions — pricing changes, product prioritization, and operational efficiency work all start with analysis this team produces. As a Data Analyst, you will work directly with product, marketing, and operations stakeholders to answer concrete business questions, build the dashboards and reports that let stakeholders self-serve afterward, and flag when the data itself looks wrong before it misleads a decision.

This is an individual-contributor role with high stakeholder visibility from day one — your analysis will regularly be presented directly to directors and above, sometimes by you.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Partner with product and operations stakeholders to translate a business question ("why did checkout conversion drop in region X last month") into a concrete, testable analysis plan.
- Write and optimize SQL queries against our data warehouse (Snowflake) to extract, validate, and shape the data needed for analysis.
- Build and maintain dashboards (we use Looker) that let non-technical stakeholders answer their own follow-up questions without needing a new analysis request every time.
- Design and analyze A/B tests, including sample size and duration planning before a test launches, not just result interpretation afterward.
- Proactively audit data pipelines for quality issues — a wrong number presented confidently is worse than no number, and catching that before it reaches a stakeholder is a core part of this role.
- Present findings clearly to audiences ranging from fellow analysts to non-technical executives, adapting the level of technical detail appropriately.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Required:
- 2+ years of experience in an analyst, data science, or closely related role, with a track record of analysis that directly informed a business decision.
- Strong SQL skills: comfortable with window functions, CTEs, and query performance considerations on large tables, not just basic joins and aggregations.
- Experience with at least one BI/visualization tool (Looker, Tableau, Power BI, or similar).
- Working knowledge of statistics sufficient to design and correctly interpret an A/B test, including common pitfalls (peeking, multiple comparisons, Simpson's paradox).
- Demonstrated attention to data quality — we will ask you to describe a time you caught a data issue others had missed.

Strongly preferred:
- Experience with Python or R for analysis beyond what SQL alone comfortably supports.
- Familiarity with dbt or similar tools for building and testing data transformation pipelines.
- Prior experience presenting analysis directly to senior stakeholders and handling pushback on a finding they didn't want to hear.

We do not require a specific academic background, degree classification, or number of years since graduation — analytical rigor and communication matter far more to us than academic pedigree.

HOW WE EVALUATE

Candidates for this role are assessed against Northfield's technical competency framework, weighted for this role toward Problem Solving & Analytical Thinking and Attention to Detail & Quality, with Technical Proficiency, Communication, Ownership & Accountability, Collaboration & Teamwork, and Adaptability & Learning Agility also scored. See the Data Analyst Rubric for exact weightings.

The process includes: an initial screen of your application materials, a SQL and analytical case-study exercise based on a realistic (anonymized) Northfield dataset, a conversation focused on how you have handled ambiguous or incorrect data in the past, and a presentation of a past analysis of your choosing to the hiring panel.

COMPENSATION AND LEVEL

This role is leveled as Analyst II under Northfield's Compensation Bands & Leveling Guide. Final level and compensation are determined based on demonstrated experience during the interview process.
