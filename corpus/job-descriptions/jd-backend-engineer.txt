NORTHFIELD DIGITAL — JOB DESCRIPTION
Role: Backend Engineer (Mid-Level)
Team: Platform Engineering
Location: Cairo, Egypt (hybrid — 3 days on-site)
Reports to: Engineering Manager, Platform

ABOUT THE ROLE

Northfield Digital's Platform Engineering team owns the core services that every product team at the company depends on: authentication, billing, notification delivery, and the internal service mesh. As a Backend Engineer on this team, you will design, build, and operate services that handle several million requests per day, with a strong emphasis on correctness, observability, and graceful failure — a bug in this layer affects every product surface at once.

This is a mid-level individual-contributor role. We expect you to work independently on well-scoped features and to contribute meaningfully to design discussions, but we do not expect you to already have staff-level architectural ownership. Growth into more senior scope is an explicit and common path from this role.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Design and implement backend services in Node.js and Go, with a strong emphasis on API contracts that are easy for other teams to depend on safely.
- Own features end-to-end: from initial design doc, through implementation and testing, to production rollout and the first weeks of monitoring afterward.
- Participate in an on-call rotation for the services you own, including writing runbooks and postmortems when something goes wrong.
- Collaborate closely with the Data Platform and Frontend teams, whose services depend on the APIs you build — you will need to communicate breaking changes and migration paths clearly.
- Contribute to the team's technical direction: our stack, our testing standards, and our incident-response practices are all still evolving, and we expect engineers at this level to have opinions and back them with evidence.
- Review other engineers' code with attention to correctness, security, and long-term maintainability, not just style.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Required:
- 3+ years of professional experience building and operating backend services in production, in any modern language (we use Node.js and Go, but strong fundamentals transfer).
- Solid understanding of relational databases (we use PostgreSQL extensively) including query performance, indexing, and transaction semantics.
- Experience with at least one message queue or event-streaming system (Kafka, SQS, RabbitMQ, or similar).
- Demonstrated ability to debug production issues independently — we will ask you to walk through a real incident you handled.
- Clear written communication — design docs and incident postmortems are core artifacts of how this team works.

Strongly preferred:
- Experience with distributed systems failure modes: partial failures, retries and idempotency, eventual consistency.
- Familiarity with container orchestration (we run on Kubernetes).
- Prior experience in a regulated or high-reliability domain (fintech, healthcare, critical infrastructure) where correctness failures have real consequences.

We do not require a specific university degree, degree classification, or number of years since graduation — we care about what you have built and can demonstrate, not where or when you studied.

HOW WE EVALUATE

Candidates for this role are assessed against Northfield's technical competency framework (see the Competency Framework document), weighted specifically for this role: Technical Proficiency, Problem Solving & Analytical Thinking, Ownership & Accountability, Collaboration & Teamwork, Communication, Adaptability & Learning Agility, and Attention to Detail & Quality. See the Backend Engineer Rubric for exact weightings and the specific evidence we look for in each area.

The process includes: an initial screen of your application materials and work history, a technical interview involving a real (anonymized, simplified) production scenario from our systems, a system design discussion, and a conversation with the hiring manager focused on ownership and collaboration examples. We do not use algorithmic puzzle-style interviews unrelated to the actual work.

COMPENSATION AND LEVEL

This role is leveled as Engineer II under Northfield's Compensation Bands & Leveling Guide. Final level and compensation are determined based on demonstrated experience during the interview process, not solely on years of experience stated in an application.
