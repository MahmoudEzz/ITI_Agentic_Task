NORTHFIELD DIGITAL — JOB DESCRIPTION
Role: Frontend Engineer (Mid-Level)
Team: Consumer Product
Location: Cairo, Egypt (hybrid — 3 days on-site)
Reports to: Engineering Manager, Consumer Product

ABOUT THE ROLE

The Consumer Product team builds and maintains the customer-facing web application used by several hundred thousand active users. As a Frontend Engineer, you will own significant parts of that application's UI layer, working closely with product designers and backend engineers to ship features that are fast, accessible, and reliable across a wide range of devices and network conditions — a meaningful share of our users are on lower-end devices and slower connections, and performance is treated as a feature, not an afterthought.

This is a mid-level individual-contributor role with room to grow into broader ownership of frontend architecture.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Build user-facing features in React and TypeScript, from design handoff through implementation, testing, and production rollout.
- Own frontend performance for the areas you work in: bundle size, render performance, and real-user monitoring metrics, not just Lighthouse scores in isolation.
- Collaborate closely with product designers, translating designs into implementation while flagging accessibility or feasibility concerns early rather than after implementation has started.
- Write and maintain component-level and end-to-end tests, and treat a flaky test as a bug to fix, not a nuisance to ignore.
- Participate in code review with a focus on maintainability and consistency across the codebase, since many engineers touch this shared frontend surface.
- Debug and resolve production frontend issues, including issues that only reproduce on specific devices, browsers, or network conditions.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Required:
- 3+ years of professional experience building production web applications with React (or a comparable component-based framework) and TypeScript.
- Solid understanding of web performance fundamentals: bundle splitting, render-blocking resources, and how to diagnose a slow page using browser devtools and real-user monitoring data.
- Experience writing automated tests for frontend code (unit, component, or end-to-end) and a clear point of view on when each type is worth the cost.
- Demonstrated attention to detail on cross-browser and cross-device behavior, not just "works on my machine."
- Comfortable working directly with product designers and giving/receiving feedback on design feasibility.

Strongly preferred:
- Experience with accessibility (WCAG) beyond a surface-level checklist — real examples of catching or fixing an accessibility issue that automated tools missed.
- Familiarity with server-side rendering or static-generation frameworks (Next.js or similar).
- Experience contributing to or maintaining a shared component library used by multiple teams.

We do not require a specific academic background, degree classification, or number of years since graduation — what you have shipped and how you think about tradeoffs matter far more than credentials.

HOW WE EVALUATE

Candidates for this role are assessed against Northfield's technical competency framework, weighted for this role toward Technical Proficiency and Attention to Detail & Quality, with Communication, Collaboration & Teamwork, Problem Solving & Analytical Thinking, Adaptability & Learning Agility, and Leadership & Initiative also scored. See the Frontend Engineer Rubric for exact weightings.

The process includes: an initial screen of your application materials and portfolio/work samples, a practical coding exercise involving a realistic UI implementation task, a discussion of a past performance or accessibility issue you diagnosed and fixed, and a conversation with the hiring manager focused on collaboration with design and cross-functional partners.

COMPENSATION AND LEVEL

This role is leveled as Engineer II under Northfield's Compensation Bands & Leveling Guide. Final level and compensation are determined based on demonstrated experience during the interview process.
